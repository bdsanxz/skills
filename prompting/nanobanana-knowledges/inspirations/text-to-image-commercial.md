## TC01 · Fragrance hero in a tonal set

`text only` · `product hero` · `4:5`

*Ideas in play:* one named concept steering set, palette and light; a key light that passes through the glass.

```text
Act as a senior fragrance art director and still-life photographer shooting the hero key visual for the launch of "AUREL — Ambre Nº 7". The image must hold up as a full-page magazine ad.

CORE IDEA: "Amber hour" — the bottle holds the last light of the day.

SUBJECT: one heavy rectangular perfume bottle in thick clear glass with softly chamfered edges, filled two-thirds with warm amber liquid, topped with a brushed-brass cap with fine vertical knurling. A small frosted label on the front reads "AUREL" in a thin high-contrast serif and "AMBRE Nº 7" below in spaced small caps. The bottle stands on a slab of honed travertine, turned 20° to camera-left.

SET: a tonal environment in amber, honey and burnt sienna. Behind the bottle, a curved plaster wall in warm sand. A single dried palm frond, out of frame, casts a long soft-edged shadow across the wall from the upper right. Nothing else in frame.

LIGHT: one low key light from camera-right at sunset height, 3200K, passing through the bottle so the liquid glows and throws an amber caustic pattern onto the travertine. A white bounce camera-left at 20% of the key keeps the label readable. A thin cool rim at 5600K traces the left edge of the glass. Lighting ratio about 4:1. No lens flare, no haze.

CAMERA: 100mm macro, f/5.6, lens slightly below the bottle's shoulder so the bottle feels monumental. Label, cap knurling and the meniscus line of the liquid are razor sharp; the wall falls gently soft.

DETAIL: fine dust on the travertine, tiny air bubbles trapped in the base of the glass, brushed grain on the cap, the frosted texture of the label, subtle film grain.

TEXT: only the label text above, perfectly spelled. No other text, no watermark.

AVOID: a floating bottle, duplicate bottles, reflections of studio equipment, plastic-looking glass, oversaturated orange, flowers or props that are not described.
```

*Riff on it:* change the concept and its palette together — "first frost" brings silver, ice blue, frosted glass and a 6500K key.

## TC02 · Embossed logo plate

`text only` · `brand materialization` · `1:1`

*Ideas in play:* the whole canvas is one material; a single effect defined by what would make it wrong; depth of field switched off on purpose.

```text
Act as a senior CGI artist and brand-identity director who specializes in logo materializations. Reference aesthetic: a hallmark struck into a foundry plate, a seal pressed into the lid of a luxury tin.

BRAND: SOLENNE, a Parisian candle house. Its mark is a circle containing a half-sun rising from a single horizontal line, with seven short straight rays.

PHASE 1 — SURFACE: the entire canvas is one continuous plate of warm champagne-gold brushed brass. Not an object on a background: one physical sheet. The brushing runs in fine concentric circles around the center, so each ring catches the light differently. Light falls off in a soft radial gradient from the center to the four edges. Fine uniform grain over the whole plate, like ISO 800 film.

PHASE 2 — THE EMBOSS: the complete filled silhouette of the SOLENNE mark rises from the plate as one solid bas-relief, like the head on a coin. Its top face is gently domed so the light rolls across it. Its walls are smooth rounded bevels. The space between the rays drops back to plate level with its own small inward bevels. The relief is thick and substantial, like a heavy medal, not a shallow bump. Proportions and silhouette exactly as described.

PHASE 3 — LIGHT: one broad, soft area light from the upper left at the 10–11 o'clock position, warm 3400K, wrapping over the bevels. A faint fill from the lower right at 10–15% of the key, just enough to hold detail in the shadow-side walls. No hard shadows, no spotlight pools, no rim light.

PHASE 4 — TYPOGRAPHY: in the lower fifth, centered on the same plate, "SOLENNE" in widely tracked geometric sans-serif capitals, and beneath it "Maison de bougies · Paris" in a light italic at half the size. Both very subtly embossed in the same brass. Nothing else.

RENDER: square 1:1. Ray-traced self-shadowing so the relief casts a real shadow on the plate. Everything in focus from edge to edge. Speculars on the ridge peaks are brilliant and tight but never blown out. No chromatic aberration, no lens distortion, no added vignette.

WHAT WOULD BE WRONG: if the mark looks like a separate 3D object resting on the plate, it is wrong. If it looks carved into the plate, it is wrong. It must look pressed outward from behind — part of the same sheet of metal.
```

*Riff on it:* the same structure becomes a blind deboss on cotton paper, a foil stamp on black card, or a mark pushed up through wet sand.

## TC03 · Inflated wildstyle type sculpture

`text only` · `3D typography` · `1:1`

*Ideas in play:* material described as physics; one color owning the whole tonal range; scale given in meters.

```text
Act as a 3D type designer working where streetwear graphics meet high-end digital sculpture.

FORM: the word "NORDLYS" as one continuous inflated sculpture. Wildstyle graffiti structure fused with balloon morphology: the letters flow into each other as one mass, each letter bulbous and pressurized, its skin stretched taut. Sharp tapered spikes erupt between and around the letters — some long and blade-like, some short like thorns — asymmetric and directional. Letters overlap and partly hide one another. Stretched membranes of the same material fill the gaps between letters. The word stays readable.

MATERIAL: one material everywhere — high-gloss latex near its bursting point. Subsurface scattering makes thin spike tips and edges glow lighter while dense volumes read darker. Large, soft, elongated speculars like on a balloon, with smaller secondary highlights in the concave zones.

COLOR: electric violet #6B2BFF is the only color. Highlights move toward a luminous near-white violet; crevices sink to the deepest saturated violet. No neutral greys, no second hue.

STAGING: a pure white #FFFFFF infinity studio with no gradient. A minimal contact shadow, so the sculpture seems to float a few centimeters up. The sculpture is wider than tall; the spikes push past its bounding box on every side, with breathing room to the canvas edges. Camera straight-on, 5–10° above center. Everything in focus. The sculpture feels about two meters wide.

RENDER: physically based, a soft white dome light at 5500K, ray-traced inter-reflections between letters, razor-clean edges against the white. Square 1:1. It reads as a product render, not as AI art.
```

*Riff on it:* swap only the material paragraph — liquid chrome, gummy candy, chunky knit wool, frosted glass — and the mood changes completely.

## TC04 · Two-zone campaign poster with a breakout

`text only` · `campaign poster` · `4:5`

*Ideas in play:* a measured photo zone over a flat color zone; the subject breaking across the cut; side-rotated micro type.

```text
Act as a senior editorial art director creating a campaign poster for RIDGELINE, an outdoor brand, launching its "Talus 3" trail-running shoe. It must work as a social post, a magazine page and a bus-shelter print.

CANVAS: vertical 4:5. A thin border in burnt orange #E4572E frames the whole poster like a collectible print. The top 60% is a photograph; the bottom 40% is a flat, solid field of the same burnt orange. The two zones meet at a hard horizontal cut — no fade.

PHOTO ZONE: a trail runner mid-stride on a sharp limestone ridge at sunrise, captured at full commitment, back knee driving forward. Camera low, looking up past the leading foot toward a vast pale sky and a distant valley in haze. Natural low sunlight from the left, long shadows, rich contrast, a punchy filmic grade with slightly lifted blacks. The runner wears a charcoal shell; the shoes are slate grey with burnt-orange laces and a chunky lugged outsole.

BREAKOUT: the runner's leading foot and shoe cross the cut line and sit on top of the orange zone, casting a soft shadow onto the flat color. About a quarter of the runner's height lies below the line. This is the key device of the poster.

GRAPHIC ZONE, all in off-white #F6F1E7, flat:
- "RIDGELINE" wordmark centered just below the breakout, 45% of the canvas width.
- Directly below, "GO WHERE IT GETS STEEP" in ultra-bold condensed capitals, tight tracking.
- Along the left edge, rotated 90° counter-clockwise: "ALL-TERRAIN · 6 MM LUGS" in small caps.
- Along the right edge, rotated 90° clockwise: "TALUS 3" in small caps.
- Bottom center, fine print: "SPRING / SUMMER 2027".
No other text. Every word perfectly spelled.

CAMERA: 35mm, f/5.6, shutter fast enough to freeze the stride with zero motion blur. Only the breakout crosses the two zones.
```

*Riff on it:* the breakout can be a drip of sauce, a wheel and its shadow, a falling croissant crumb, a dancer's hand.

## TC05 · Screen-portal electronics ad

`text only` · `tech campaign` · `1:1`

```text

THE PORTAL: on screen, a photographic alpine valley — snow peaks, a turquoise glacial lake, dark spruce forest, morning mist. The lake flows out through the bottom edge of the screen and becomes the glossy foreground floor; two or three wet stones and a thin mist mark the transition. One mountain ridge rises slightly past the top bezel. It stays photographic and physically believable, not fantasy VFX. Cool daylight from the landscape spills onto the bezel, the floor and the nearest wall.

ENVIRONMENT: a near-future gallery of matte graphite panels, black glass and brushed aluminium, with a polished dark floor and realistic reflection falloff. Thin cool-white architectural light lines lead toward the television. Light haze, deep shadows, generous negative space.

LAYERS: foreground — the escaping lake, stones and mist. Midground — the television, slightly right of center, filling 55–65% of the frame. Background — giant typography and architecture, softening with distance.

TYPOGRAPHY: behind the television, "BEYOND THE FRAME" in an enormous black-weight geometric grotesk, all caps, tight kerning, soft white with a faint silver tone, flat with no bevel, covering about 35% of the canvas. The television and the mountain ridge hide parts of the letters; the outer letters crop at the canvas edges. Lower left, "See every detail. Feel every moment." in a light sans. Under it, in tiny tracked caps, "4K HDR • QUANTUM COLOR • 120 HZ". Lower right, "DISCOVER MORE →" in small caps. No other text.

PALETTE: obsidian #080A0D and graphite #181C22 for the environment, soft white #F3F6F8 for type, cyan #27D7FF only in the architectural light lines. The landscape supplies all the natural color.

CAMERA AND LIGHT: cinema-camera look, 35mm lens, slightly low three-quarter view from 80 cm above the floor, the television fully sharp and the far architecture softening. A large diffused overhead key, a cool rim on the frame edges, an accurate contact shadow under the stand.

AVOID: cyberpunk, neon glow around every object, holograms, a spaceship interior, gaming aesthetics, a curved or warped screen, thick bezels, fake ports, a floating television, heavy lens flare, misspelled or warped letters, extra headlines.
```

## TC16 · Brand embossed plate
