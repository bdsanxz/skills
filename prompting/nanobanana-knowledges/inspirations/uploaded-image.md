# Uploaded image as source

One image is uploaded and becomes the source material for a new image: a product photo becomes a campaign, a selfie becomes a headshot, a logo becomes an object, a sketch becomes a render. The upload's identity is kept; almost everything around it is new. To *edit* the upload while keeping its scene, see `editing-transformation.md`.

Entries with `[SLOTS]` list an example value for each slot.

---

## U02 · Phone snapshot to clean e-commerce packshot

`1 upload: casual product photo` · `packshot` · `1:1`

*Ideas in play:* identify, isolate, clean, correct; a white backdrop with a real contact shadow.

```text
Identify the main product in the uploaded photo. Recreate it as a professional e-commerce packshot:
- Remove any hands, fingers, table, clutter and background.
- Place the product on a pure white seamless background (RGB 255, 255, 255), centered, filling about 75% of the frame.
- Add a soft, natural contact shadow directly beneath it.
- Correct lens distortion and perspective so vertical edges are straight.
- Color-correct to neutral 5500K daylight; true-to-life colors.
- Clean dust and small scratches on the product only; keep its real texture, seams and printed text exactly as they are.
Soft, even commercial studio lighting with a gentle highlight that reveals the material. 1:1. No added text, props or watermark.
```

## U05 · Logo revealed through torn street posters

`1 upload: logo` · `brand street art` · `4:5`

*Ideas in play:* the logo emerges as negative space from layered decay; sections for material, physics, surface and atmosphere.

```text
Transform the uploaded logo into a realistic urban wall where the logo's silhouette is revealed through layers of torn posters. The logo keeps its exact shape and proportions, appearing where years of posters have been ripped away.

MATERIALS: many overlapping poster layers of different colors, prints and paper weights; ripped edges with fibrous texture, curls and loose fragments; aged paper with fading, stains, glue residue and peeling.
PHYSICS: irregular tears of varying thickness; edges curling outward with humidity; glue holding some sections while others hang loose; deeper, older layers visible in places.
SURFACE: rough concrete under the posters, glue marks, cracks, dirt and weathering on exposed wall.
ENVIRONMENT: an alley wall on a city corner, with hints of graffiti and other posters at the edges. Not staged, not clean.
LIGHT: natural overcast daylight; shadows inside the torn layers; small highlights on curled edges.
CAMERA: straight-on or slightly angled close-up; the logo clearly readable; depth from overlapping paper.

Raw, layered, time-worn, authentic. Hyper-real urban surface photography. Vertical 4:5. No text overlays.
```

## U16 · Product photo to campaign poster with a headline

`1 upload: product` · `campaign poster` · `[ASPECT_RATIO]`

*Ideas in play:* the identity lock opens and closes; a measured two-zone canvas gives the headline its own clean field; the only words allowed are quoted.

*Slots:* `[BRAND]` Tallgrass Outfitters · `[PRODUCT_TYPE]` matte black stainless-steel vacuum flask · `[SCENE]` a frost-covered granite boulder above a misty pine valley at sunrise, a coiled climbing rope, a few frosted pine needles · `[STYLING]` its lid off, a thin plume of steam rising into the cold air · `[LIGHT]` cold low sun from camera-right at about 4500K, drawing a hard rim along the steel · `[FIELD_COLOR]` pine green #1F3B2D · `[TYPE_COLOR]` frost white #F2F4F1 · `[HEADLINE]` Warm at the summit · `[ASPECT_RATIO]` 4:5

```text
Image 1 is the product reference: a [PRODUCT_TYPE] by [BRAND]. Preserve its exact shape, proportions, color, material, surface pattern, finish and any mark or printed word — no redesign, no smoothing, no changes at all.

Create a [ASPECT_RATIO] campaign poster for [BRAND]. The top 65–70% of the canvas is a photograph; the rest is a flat solid field of [FIELD_COLOR]. The two zones meet at a clean horizontal cut, no fade.

PHOTOGRAPH: the product from Image 1, [STYLING], in [SCENE]. The product is the clear hero, about half the canvas height, turned slightly to show its form; props sit around it, never touching or covering it. Exactly one product in frame.

LIGHT: [LIGHT] as the key, soft and large; a white bounce on the shadow side at about 20% of the key; a faint rim tracing the product's edge. The light reveals texture and material. One accurate contact shadow. 50mm, f/4, the product razor sharp, the background softly out of focus, fine film grain.

TEXT, exactly as written: "[HEADLINE]" in a large, warm editorial serif in [TYPE_COLOR], centered in the solid field, about 70% of the canvas width. No other text, no logo, no watermark. Every letter perfectly spelled.

AVOID: a second or floating product, a warped shape, plastic-looking materials, clutter over the product, a misspelled or distorted headline.

Final check: the product is identical to Image 1, and "[HEADLINE]" is the only text in the image.
```

*Riff on it:* drop the solid field and set the headline over a calm area of the photo, or let a piece of the product break across the cut onto the color field, as in TC04.
