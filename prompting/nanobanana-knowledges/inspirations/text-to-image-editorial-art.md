# Text to image: editorial, cinematic and art

Nothing is uploaded. Portraits, cinematic scenes, illustration, 3D characters, concept art, design pieces and surreal ideas. Every person and character here is fictional. Finished prompts come first; slot templates with `[SLOTS]` follow at the end.

## TA01 · Environmental portrait on film

`text only` · `editorial portrait` · `4:5`

*Ideas in play:* a person defined by their work and hands; a film stock and a window as the whole lighting plan.

```text
An environmental editorial portrait of a woman in her late sixties who repairs antique clocks, photographed in her narrow workshop for a weekend magazine feature. She sits at a scarred oak workbench, loupe pushed up onto her forehead, holding a tiny brass gear between tweezers and looking straight into the lens with calm, amused attention. Silver hair pulled back loosely, a few strands escaping; a faded indigo work apron over a cream linen shirt with rolled sleeves; small burn scars and ink on her fingertips.

Around her: dozens of clock faces on the wall behind, all stopped at different times; a green-shaded bench lamp; jars of screws; a half-disassembled carriage clock with its movement exposed. The wall is out of focus but readable.

Light: one tall window on camera-left at late afternoon, soft and directional, the far side of her face falling into gentle shadow; the bench lamp adds a small warm pool on her hands.

Shot on a medium-format film camera with an 80mm lens at f/2.8, Kodak Portra 400, natural grain, slightly lifted shadows, warm but honest skin tones. Natural skin texture with visible pores and fine wrinkles, not retouched; natural catchlights in the eyes. Vertical 4:5. No text.
```

*Riff on it:* a ferryman at dawn, a ceramicist covered in slip, a night-shift baker in flour light — the job supplies every prop.

## TA02 · Cinematic wide: lone figure, vast scale

`text only` · `cinematic still` · `21:9`

*Ideas in play:* tiny subject against enormous geography; atmosphere doing the storytelling; a color grade named like a colorist would.

```text
A cinematic 21:9 widescreen film still. A lone traveler in a long rust-red coat walks along the spine of an enormous salt flat toward a colossal, half-buried stone hand rising from the white ground — its fingers 80 meters tall, weathered and cracked, one finger broken off and lying in pieces nearby. The traveler is tiny, placed on the left third, leaving a long shadow that stretches toward the hand.

The sky is a heavy pale overcast with a single break where low sun spills through, catching the tips of the fingers in warm gold while the rest of the scene stays cool. A thin layer of water on the salt mirrors the sky and the hand. Faint dust devils far in the distance.

Anamorphic lens look: a slight horizontal flare from the sun break, oval bokeh, gentle barrel distortion at the frame edges. Color grade: muted teal-grey shadows, warm highlights, low saturation except the coat. Fine 35mm film grain. Deep focus from the traveler to the hand.

No text, no letterbox bars, no logos.
```

*Riff on it:* swap the monument (a stranded ocean liner in a desert, a fallen satellite in a rice field); keep the scale gap enormous.

## TA03 · Isometric miniature diorama

`text only` · `3D diorama` · `1:1`

*Ideas in play:* a whole world on a cut-out block; tactile miniature materials; dozens of tiny stories rewarding a second look.

```text
A 45° isometric miniature diorama of a tiny ramen shop on a rainy Tokyo back-street corner, built on a square block of earth cut cleanly at the sides to show soil, pipes and a subway tunnel beneath. The shop has a red noren curtain, steamed-up windows, a glowing paper lantern and a counter with six stools; inside, a chef ladles broth while two customers slurp noodles. Outside: a vending machine glowing blue, a stack of beer crates, a bicycle with a basket, a cat sheltering under the eaves, a salaryman with a clear umbrella, puddles reflecting the lantern, overhead power lines with tiny raindrops.

Materials look handmade: polymer-clay figures with fingerprints of texture, balsa-wood beams, printed paper signs, resin puddles. Soft overcast top light plus the warm interior glow; tilt-shift macro photography with a shallow focal plane so the shop is sharp and the block's corners soften.

Plain warm-grey studio background with a soft shadow under the block. Square 1:1. The only lettering is the small Japanese shop sign "ラーメン" on the lantern. No other text.
```

*Riff on it:* a lighthouse keeper's island, a Moroccan rooftop at dusk, a space-station greenhouse — always cut the block to show what is underground.

## TA04 · Picture-book spread

`text only` · `children's illustration` · `16:9`

*Ideas in play:* a named traditional medium and paper; a page layout with reserved space for text; a gentle emotional beat.

```text
A double-page spread for a children's picture book, 16:9, painted in gouache and colored pencil on warm, slightly toothy watercolor paper, with visible brush texture and pencil hatching in the shadows.

Scene: a small badger in a yellow raincoat and oversized boots stands at the edge of a moonlit pond, holding a jam jar with a single firefly inside, about to let it go. On the right page, the pond reflects a sky full of other fireflies drifting over tall reeds and lily pads; a heron sleeps on one leg in the background. The badger's expression is brave but a little sad.

Palette: deep blue-violet night, moss greens, the warm yellow of the raincoat and the firefly glow as the only warm light. The glow lights the badger's face and paws from below.

Composition: the badger on the left third of the left page; the upper left quarter of the left page is calm, empty night sky reserved for text. Soft, rounded shapes, gentle edges, no harsh black outlines. No text, no lettering, no gutter line drawn.
```

*Riff on it:* change the medium for a different shelf — linocut with three inks, risograph in pink and teal, cut-paper collage.

## TA05 · Collectible designer-toy character

`text only` · `3D character render` · `4:5`

*Ideas in play:* a toy described by its material, joints and packaging cues; an original character with a clear silhouette.

```text
A studio product render of an original collectible vinyl designer toy named Tuck, about 18 cm tall: a round-bodied sea otter wearing a chunky mustard-yellow knitted fisherman's sweater and a tiny red beanie, holding a scallop shell like a coffee cup. ## TA06 · Sci-fi concept-art keyframe
```

`text only` · `concept art` · `16:9`

*Ideas in play:* a production-art brief with scale cues, readable silhouettes and a value structure.

```text
A production concept-art keyframe for an original science-fiction film, 16:9, digital painting with confident brushwork and photographic detail in the focal area.

Scene: a cargo hauler the size of a city block descends through monsoon clouds toward a floating harbor built on the backs of giant tethered kelp balloons above a storm-dark ocean. Cranes on the harbor reach up; tiny figures in orange suits stand on a catwalk in the foreground for scale. Rain sheets slant across the frame; lightning lights the underside of the clouds.

Design language: the ship is heavy, utilitarian and asymmetrical, with exposed ribs, sun-bleached cargo pods and hazard stripes. The harbor is lightweight and organic, rope bridges and translucent balloon skins glowing from inside.

Value structure: dark foreground silhouettes, a mid-value storm, the brightest value at the ship's landing lights and the glowing balloons. Palette: slate blue, storm green, warm sodium-orange accents.

Camera: low, from the foreground catwalk, 24mm, slight upward tilt, strong atmospheric perspective. No text, no UI, no signature.
```

*Riff on it:* the same brief structure for fantasy (a cathedral grown from coral), horror (a lighthouse whose beam is dark), or solarpunk.

## TA07 · Macro nature with a story

`text only` · `nature macro` · `4:5`

*Ideas in play:* a tiny drama at macro scale; optics that justify every bit of the blur.

```text
A macro wildlife photograph of a jumping spider perched on the rim of a dew-covered rose leaf at dawn, looking directly into the lens with all four front eyes, a single water droplet balanced on the leaf's edge in front of it reflecting the entire sunrise garden upside-down. The spider's body is covered in iridescent green and copper scales and fine white hairs; its pedipalps are raised as if curious.

```

*Riff on it:* a snail crossing a fallen cherry blossom, a frost-crusted bumblebee waking on a crocus, a hummingbird's tongue mid-sip.

## TA08 · Surreal visual metaphor

`text only` · `conceptual photograph` · `4:5`

*Ideas in play:* an abstract idea made literal with miniature scale; a deadpan photographic treatment that makes it believable.

```text
A conceptual editorial photograph that works as a visual metaphor for burnout — the idea is shown, never written. Extreme macro view of an ordinary wooden matchstick standing upright on a desk, its head burned down to black ash; on the charred head, a tiny business-suited figurine (model-railway scale, 1:87) sits slumped with its briefcase beside it, staring out. Thin smoke still curls from the ash.

Around the match, out of focus: the edge of a laptop keyboard, a coffee ring on the desk, a pile of other unburned matches neatly lined up like a to-do list.

Light: a single hard desk-lamp light from the upper right at 3000K, a deep shadow on the left, the smoke backlit. Hasselblad-style medium-format macro, 120mm, f/8, the figurine and ash razor sharp. Muted palette, almost monochrome except the warm lamp. Deadpan, quiet, slightly funny. Vertical 4:5. No text.
```

*Riff on it:* "information overload" as a tiny librarian buried under falling paper confetti; "deadline" as a figurine sprinting on the second hand of a clock.

## TA09 · Fashion editorial in a landscape

`text only` · `fashion editorial` · `3:4`

*Ideas in play:* garment, body and land answering each other in shape and color; a precise photographic language.

```text
A high-fashion editorial photograph for an autumn issue, 3:4. A model with a shaved head and strong brows stands on a black volcanic beach in Iceland, wearing a monumental sculptural coat of rust-orange wool that billows sideways in the wind like a sail, its hem whipping into a long curve. Underneath, a fitted black turtleneck and wide charcoal trousers. Her pose is still, chin lifted, eyes closed against the wind.

Behind her, grey-green Atlantic waves break into white foam; a single sea stack stands in the mist on the right; the sky is a flat silver overcast.

Shot on medium format, 45mm, f/5.6, from slightly low; the model on the left third with the coat spreading across the center. Soft natural overcast light plus a subtle silver reflector; a cool, desaturated grade where the coat is the only saturated color. Fine grain, crisp wool fibers, salt spray in the air. No text, no logos.
```

*Riff on it:* a pleated silver gown on a salt flat at noon; a quilted coat in a snowy birch forest under blue-hour light.

## TA10 · Interior design visualization

`text only` · `interior` · `16:9`

*Ideas in play:* a defined design language and material palette; daylight as the main character; a lived-in detail or two.

```text
A photorealistic interior visualization of a Japandi living room in a converted 1930s warehouse, 16:9. Tall steel-framed windows on the left let in soft morning sun that falls in long rectangles across a pale oak floor. A low boucle sofa in oatmeal, a round travertine coffee table, a wabi-sabi ceramic vase holding a single branch of quince blossom, a paper floor lamp, and a low walnut sideboard with a record player and a stack of vinyl. ```
```

## TA11 · Typographic illustration

`text only` · `typography art` · `1:1`

*Ideas in play:* letters are the only building material; legibility kept while the shape reads.

```text
A typographic illustration in the shape of a sperm whale diving downward, built only from the letters of the phrase "THE DEEP REMEMBERS EVERYTHING", repeated and fitted to the silhouette. The letters bend, stretch and change weight to follow the whale's curves: heavy black condensed capitals for the massive head, lighter and smaller letters tapering through the body, the tail flukes made of two flared "E"s. No extra lines or shapes; the whale is legible as a whale and the phrase is legible as text when read along the body.

Color: letters in deep navy #0B1F3A on a pale sea-foam background #E3F1EE, with a few letters in the head picked out in coral #F26B4F like barnacles. Clean vector style, crisp edges, generous margin. Square 1:1. No other text.
```

*Riff on it:* a coffee cup made of a café's opening hours, a bicycle made of a cycling club's motto, a city skyline made of its street names.

## TA12 · Character design sheet

`text only` · `character sheet` · `16:9`

*Ideas in play:* a production-ready sheet with views, expressions, details and a palette, all locked to one design.

```text
A professional character design sheet for an original animated-film character, 16:9, clean light-grey background, consistent 2D style with soft cel shading.

CHARACTER: Ines, an 11-year-old apprentice lighthouse keeper. Curly dark hair tied up with a strip of red sailcloth, a freckled brown face, a too-big yellow oilskin coat with rolled cuffs, a brass spyglass on a leather strap, patched navy trousers, rubber boots with one lace replaced by string.

LAYOUT:
- Left: turnaround in four views — front, three-quarter, side, back — standing on one baseline, same height.
- Top right: an expression sheet of six heads — curious, laughing, stubborn, frightened, sleepy, proud.
- Middle right: three action poses — climbing a ladder, bracing against wind, peering through the spyglass.
- Bottom right: close-up details of the spyglass, the boots and the sailcloth hair tie, plus a palette strip of six color swatches.

Every view keeps the same proportions (about 1:5 head-to-body), the same costume and the same colors. Short labels in a clean sans under each section: "TURNAROUND", "EXPRESSIONS", "POSES", "DETAILS". No other text.
```

*Riff on it:* a creature sheet with a skeleton overlay and a scale figure; a prop sheet for a magical object.

## TA13 · Vintage travel poster

*Ideas in play:* a historical print process as the style; limited inks; type designed into the image.


## TA14 · Dutch still life with food

`text only` · `fine-art food photo` · `4:5`

*Ideas in play:* an art-historical lighting tradition used for a modern subject; carefully placed decay and texture.

```text
A fine-art food photograph in the style of a 17th-century Dutch still-life painting, vertical 4:5. On a dark oak table partly covered with a rumpled pewter-grey linen cloth: a half-eaten wheel of aged cheese with a knife stuck in it, a cut pomegranate spilling seeds, a tilted pewter plate holding oysters on crushed ice, a crystal goblet of red wine catching light, a lemon with its peel spiralling off the table edge, grapes with a bloom on their skins, and one fly resting on the rim of the plate.

Light: a single soft window light from the upper left, everything else falling into deep near-black shadow (chiaroscuro); warm highlights, rich glazed color in the midtones.

Medium-format photograph, 90mm, f/8, every surface rich in texture: pewter scratches, the lemon's pores, wax on the cheese rind, condensation on the ice. No text.
```

*Riff on it:* modern junk food styled with the same reverence — a convenience-store dinner shot like a Rembrandt.
